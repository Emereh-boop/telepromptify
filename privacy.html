Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Summary

Telepromptify runs in your browser or app. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your script, settings and recordings are not sent to us or anyone else.

Who we are

Telepromptify is operated by Sorzi Enterprise, Nigeria. Contact: sorzibrand@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

  • Your script text and settings (font size, speed, camera and color options) are stored in your browser local storage so they are there next time. You can erase them any time with “Delete my data” on the script screen, or by clearing site data.
  • Recordings are made on your device and are only saved when you press Save. We never receive them.
  • Imported files (.txt, .docx, .pdf) are read on your device and are not uploaded.

Camera and microphone

Telepromptify asks for camera and microphone permission to record you. They are active only on the teleprompter screen and are switched off when you leave it. You can revoke permission in your browser or Android settings.

Cookies

Telepromptify does not set cookies and does not use advertising or tracking technologies. The local storage described above is strictly necessary to remember your script and settings.

Hosting logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) may process technical data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and secure the site. We do not use this data to identify you.

Children

Telepromptify is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. We collect no personal data at all.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export or delete on a server. Under laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the GDPR you may still contact us with any question or complaint.

Changes

If this changes, we will update this page and its date.
